# ResourceShift AI - Brand & Design Guidelines

> **Visual Identity**: Clean, Modern, Trustworthy Sustainability Intelligence  
> **Audience**: University Facility Directors, Energy Managers, Sustainability Officers  
> **Compliance Target**: WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast Ratios (Minimum 4.5:1 for body text)

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Tone

ResourceShift AI balances **environmental stewardship** with **engineering precision**. The interface should convey:
- **Authority & Accuracy**: Grounded in deterministic physics and telemetry, not speculative estimations.
- **Clarity & Actionability**: Immediate answers to *"Where is power being wasted?"* and *"What action should I approve right now?"*
- **Eco-Modern Aesthetic**: Deep slates paired with vibrant emerald accents and warm alert ambers—avoiding generic flat greens or dull corporate gray.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Primary Sans-Serif**: `Inter`, `Outfit`, or system-ui fallback (neutral, geometric, highly legible at small sizes).
- **Monospace Telemetry Font**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` (for kWh metrics, timestamps, and dollar savings).

| Element | Size | Weight | Line Height | Example Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display / Hero** | `text-3xl` (30px) | `font-bold` | `leading-tight` | Building Overview Header |
| **Section Title** | `text-xl` (20px) | `font-semibold` | `leading-snug` | "Active Anomalies Detected" |
| **KPI Stat Value** | `text-2xl` (24px) | `font-mono font-bold` | `leading-none` | `1,248 kWh` / `$174.72` |
| **Body Text** | `text-sm` (14px) | `font-normal` | `leading-relaxed` | Root-cause analysis narrative |
| **Metadata & Badges** | `text-xs` (12px) | `font-medium` | `leading-normal` | "Floors 2-3 HVAC", "Confidence 94%" |

## 4. Spacing, Grid & Layout System

- **Grid Unit**: Standard 8pt increments (`p-2`, `p-4`, `p-6`, `gap-4`, `gap-6`).
- **Dashboard Layout**:
  - Top Bar: Building Selector (`Engineering Hall - North Wing`), Status Indicator (🟢 Live Monitoring), Current Time (After-hours mode badge).
  - Row 1: KPI Stat Grid (Current Demand kW, After-Hours Waste kWh, Recoverable $, Carbon Offset kg).
  - Row 2 (Main split): Telemetry Area Graph (2/3 width) + Anomaly & HITL Proposal Feed (1/3 width).
  - Row 3: Action History & Audit Log (Full width table).
- **Corner Radius**:
  - Cards: `rounded-xl` (12px) with subtle 1px border (`border border-slate-700/60`).
  - Badges & Buttons: `rounded-lg` (8px).
  - Status Pills: `rounded-full`.

## 5. Component Design Conventions

### 1. KPI Metric Card
```tsx

<div className="bg-slate-800/80 border border-slate-700/60 rounded-xl p-5 shadow-sm">
  <div className="flex items-center justify-between text-xs text-slate-400 font-medium">
    <span>After-Hours Excess</span>
    <AlertTriangle className="w-4 h-4 text-amber-400" />
  </div>
  <div className="mt-2 text-2xl font-mono font-bold text-slate-50">342 kWh</div>
  <div className="mt-1 text-xs text-amber-400 flex items-center gap-1">
    +28% above after-hours baseline
  </div>
</div>
```

### 2. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Action Proposal Card
Every proposal generated by Gemini must feature:
- **Title**: Plain-language action (e.g., *"Set Floor 3 HVAC to Setback Mode"*).
- **Impact Badge**: Estimated savings (`$48.50 | 340 kWh | 142 kg CO2`).
- **Reasoning Brief**: Collapsible explanation citing schedule + sensor data.
- **Explicit Decision CTA**: Two distinct buttons—**"Approve Shift"** (Emerald) and **"Dismiss"** (Ghost slate).
- *Never include auto-execution timers without human signoff.*

### 3. Telemetry Graph Styling
- **Baseline Curve**: Dashed sky-blue line (`#0284C7`, dashed).
- **Actual Load Area**: Gradient filled area under curve transitioning from vibrant mint (`#10B981`) during normal hours to warm amber (`#F59E0B`) during detected anomalous spikes.
- **Reference Zones**: Shaded vertical band denoting official college after-hours window (e.g., 19:00 - 06:00).

## 6. Accessibility & Usability Checklist

- [x] All text maintains WCAG 2.1 AA contrast against its respective card surface.
- [x] Never rely on color alone to convey anomaly severity—always pair with icons (`AlertTriangle`, `CheckCircle2`) and explicit text labels.
- [x] Numbers formatted with localized separators (`1,240.50 kWh`, `$182.00`).
- [x] Interactive buttons and cards maintain clear `:focus-visible` focus rings (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-emerald-500`).
